# Whatsit Swap

A trading table for weird, random, one-of-a-kind things. Built for Aria.

Live: https://rasjammin.github.io/whatsit-swap/

Two traders, one phone laid flat between them. Tap a thing in your stash to put
it on the table, tap it again to take it back. Then each trader has three round
buttons:

- **Green circle, check - Keep playing** - not enough yet, the other trader
  should put more stuff down.
- **Blue circle, check - Yes, finish** - I'm happy. When *both* traders tap
  blue, the stuff swaps.
- **Red circle, X - Cancel trade** - call the whole thing off, everybody gets
  their stuff back.

Every item is randomly generated - a name, a weird fact, a rarity (1-5 stars)
and its own serial number, so no two are quite the same. Tap **Find stuff** to
dig up more. Names, animal pictures and stashes all save in the browser.

The whole game is sized to one phone screen: nothing scrolls except the two
stashes.

Single file, no dependencies: `index.html`.
